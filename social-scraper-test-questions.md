# Social Media Scraper: Data Quality Test Questions

40 ready-to-run questions, 10 per question type.

| Question type | What is measured |
|---|---|
| Trend detection | Whether the trends identified are genuine and growing |
| Sentiment tracking | Whether sentiment is classified correctly |
| Community discovery | Whether the communities identified are relevant to the question |
| Competitive mapping | Whether the brands identified are genuine competitors or comparisons |

---

## 1. Trend Detection

*Team members review the top trends for a sample of questions and check them against the collected posts*

1. What topics related to Nike have been growing the fastest on social media over the last 30 days?
2. Is the conversation about Starbucks increasing or decreasing compared to the previous quarter?
3. What new hashtags are people using alongside Spotify this month that weren't popular 3 months ago?
4. Which seasonal topics around Starbucks show up every year, and are they building up right now?
5. In Toronto, what are people talking about most when they mention Tim Hortons, and is it growing?
6. Which Tesla features are getting more mentions week over week?
7. Is there a spike in mentions of Lululemon in the last 7 days, and is it organic or driven by a single viral post?
8. What emerging topics in the fast fashion space (Zara, H&M, Shein) are gaining traction on TikTok and Reddit right now?
9. Which cities are showing the biggest rise in Airbnb mentions compared to last year?
10. Are mentions of pricing rising or falling for Netflix over the last 6 months?

---

## 2. Sentiment Tracking

*Team members hand-label a sample of posts and compare their labels with Voyager’s*

1. What is the overall sentiment toward Apple on social media this month?
2. How has sentiment toward Netflix changed since its last subscription price increase?
3. What are the most common complaints people post about Air Canada?
4. What do people praise most about Costco?
5. Is sentiment toward Tesla different on Reddit, X, and Instagram?
6. How do people feel about McDonald's latest menu launch?
7. Which posts about Uber are sarcastic, and are they classified as negative rather than positive?
8. What is the sentiment toward Starbucks in Toronto compared to Vancouver?
9. How do people feel about IKEA's pricing compared to their feelings about its quality?
10. Are there posts about Amazon with mixed sentiment (e.g. loves fast delivery, hates returns), and are they handled correctly?

---

## 3. Community Discovery

*Team members review the top-ranked communities for a sample of questions*

1. Which online communities talk about Nike sneakers the most?
2. Which subreddits are most relevant to coffee lovers in Toronto?
3. Who are the top creators discussing Lululemon on TikTok and Instagram?
4. Which Facebook groups or Discord servers are active around Tesla owners?
5. Where do people go to ask for recommendations on budget airlines?
6. Which communities are made up of Spotify's target audience (e.g. students, gym-goers, commuters)?
7. Are there niche communities that talk about Patagonia but aren't well known?
8. Which hashtags do the most engaged Starbucks fans use?
9. Which communities are talking about Shein negatively, and what are they focused on?
10. Which online communities are most active in Toronto when it comes to food delivery apps (Uber Eats, DoorDash, SkipTheDishes)?

---

## 4. Competitive Mapping

*Team members review the brands identified for a sample of questions*

1. Which brands do people compare Nike to most often?
2. Who are Netflix's top 5 competitors based on social media conversation?
3. Which brands do people switch to when they leave Starbucks?
4. Which competitors are gaining more mentions than Tesla this quarter?
5. In Toronto, which brands compete with Tim Hortons for the same customers?
6. What do people say Adidas does better than Nike?
7. What do people say Nike does better than Adidas?
8. Which brands are mentioned together with Airbnb in "best of" or "alternatives to" posts?
9. Are there any new or emerging competitors people are starting to mention alongside Spotify?
10. Which brands show up in Tesla conversations but are not real competitors (e.g. a supplier, a charging network, or an unrelated brand with a similar name)?

---

## Suggested scoring

For each question, mark the result as **Pass**, **Partial**, or **Fail** against the check under its section header. Worth logging per run: platform, date range, number of posts collected, and any irrelevant or duplicate results.
